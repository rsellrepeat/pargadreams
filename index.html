Location

Parga, Epirus —
Greece's Best-Kept Secret

Buying property in Parga, Greece puts you on the Ionian coast of Epirus — transparent water, the landmark Venetian castle, pine-backed beaches at Valtos and Lichnos, and 280+ sunny days per year. The villas sit on the Trikorfo hillside above Parga, with direct access to hiking and cycling trails into the Epirus mountains — and a historic fortress ruin within five minutes’ walk. The mountain panoramas visible from every terrace set Trikorfo apart: the Epirus mountains are the view here, and they are extraordinary. With Greece welcoming a record 37 million visitors in 2025, generating €23 billion in revenue, Parga's investment credentials are stronger than ever.
