How to Buy — New Build Villa Parga Greece

Buying in 6 Steps

Buying a new-build villa for sale in Parga, Greece follows a clear process. For international buyers, the entire purchase can be handled through a local lawyer with power of attorney — no requirement to be in Greece for most steps.

01
Inquiry & Reservation
Confirm availability, agree price. Sign reservation agreement and pay deposit (typically 5–10%). Villa removed from market.
02
Greek Tax Number (AFM)
All buyers must obtain a Greek Tax ID before purchase. Obtainable via a lawyer with power of attorney — no visit required.
03
Engage a Property Lawyer
Your independent lawyer conducts due diligence: title search, building permit, planning compliance, contract review.
04
Due Diligence Period
Land Registry verification, planning compliance confirmation, energy certificate review. Typically 6–8 weeks for new builds.
05
Pay Transfer Tax
Property Transfer Tax: 3.09% of assessed value. Capital Gains Tax suspended until 31 December 2026 — verify with tax advisor.
06
Sign Notarial Deed
Final deed signed before a Greek notary (in person or via PoA lawyer). Deed registered at Land Registry — ownership transfers to you.

Greek Golden Visa — Zone 2

Golden Visa
Eligible at €400,000

Parga, Epirus falls within Zone 2 of the Greek Golden Visa programme, where the qualifying threshold is €400,000. At €450,000, Pasha Villas exceeds this threshold. The programme grants a 5-year renewable EU Schengen Area residency permit for the investor and their immediate family.

Important: Current Greek law prohibits Golden Visa properties from being used for short-term rental (Airbnb, Booking.com). Buyers planning to use the villa for rental income should seek independent legal advice. Always consult a licensed Greek immigration attorney for current rules before making any investment decision based on residency considerations.
