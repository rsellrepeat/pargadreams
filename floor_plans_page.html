Every Centimetre Considered.

These are the official architectural floor plans for a standard Pasha Villa unit as designed by Anastasios Efstratiou. Each of the six villas in the complex follows this two-level layout — a generous ground floor planned around indoor-outdoor flow, and a quieter loft level above for sleeping.

The footprint of 6.85 m × 11.5 m = 78.775 m² ground floor area confirms the declared specification of 78.78 m² exactly. The loft adds 35.28 m² for a total of 114.06 m² of living space — before accounting for the private pool terrace, covered pergola, and private parking, which are all included in the purchase price of €450,000.
